# Orchestra — UG Personal Examination Timetable Assistant

**Orchestra** is an intelligent examination timetable assistant designed exclusively for students at the **University of Ghana**. It simplifies exam season by automatically generating a clean, personalized, and chronologically organized examination schedule tailored specifically to your registered courses.

---

## What Orchestra Does

Managing examination schedules across long institutional timetables can be stressful and error-prone. Orchestra solves this by consolidating your entire exam schedule into a single, beautifully organized view.

- **Search via STS Timetable App**: Enter your Student Index Number and registered course codes to instantly find your assigned examination dates, times, campuses, and exact allocated venue ranges.
- **Upload Timetable PDF**: Have an official examination timetable PDF? Simply upload the document to extract and organize your personal exam schedule without needing your student ID.
- **Real-Time Next Examination Highlight**: Automatically identifies and displays your next upcoming paper based on the current date and time, so you always know what paper to prepare for next.
- **University of Ghana Aesthetics**: Designed in the regal Navy and Gold palette of the University of Ghana (*Integri Procedamus*).
- **Print & Save Options**: Generate a clean, 1-page paper printout or download a formatted personal PDF copy to keep on your phone or computer.
